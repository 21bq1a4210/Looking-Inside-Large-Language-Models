Glossary

Transformer

A neural network architecture that excels at handling sequential data like text. It's the foundation of models like GPT and BERT.

Tokenization

The process of breaking down a piece of text into smaller units called "tokens." These can be words, parts of words, or characters.

Embedding

Converting tokens into numerical vectors. This allows the model to work with the mathematical representation of words and their meanings.

Self-Attention

A mechanism that allows the model to weigh the importance of different tokens in the input text when processing a specific token. It helps in understanding context.

Feedforward Network

A component within each Transformer block that processes the output from the self-attention layer, allowing for more complex computations.

LM Head (Language Model Head)

The final layer of the model that takes the processed information and generates a probability distribution over the entire vocabulary to predict the most likely next token.
